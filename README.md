# Revora

Revora turns public Google reviews into practical customer insights for local businesses. A single complaint can be noise; repeated complaints about wait times, booking, or service handoffs point to something a team can fix.

## The problem
Reviews are scattered across listings. Reading them one by one makes it hard to spot recurring issues, compare patterns, and see whether a change helped.

## What I built
I built a product that lets a business select its public review evidence, find repeated themes, compare relevant context, and track how findings change over time. It gives teams a clearer next step without posting to their listings automatically. A free preview offers a limited sample; a paid plan expands the analysis and history.

## Early traction
60+ free users and 120+ reviews processed (figures I shared in September 2026).
